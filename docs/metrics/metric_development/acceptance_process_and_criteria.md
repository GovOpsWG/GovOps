# Acceptance Process and Criteria — Draft

> Starter proposal for discussion. This workflow is not yet agreed and does not replace the existing [contribution guidance](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md) or [governance policy](../../../GOVERNANCE.md).

## Proposed stages

- **Pre-proposed:** Explore an idea in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/GovOpsWG/GovOps/discussions), including its purpose, potential benefit and available evidence.
- **Proposed:** Develop a definition using the [draft template](definition_template.md), add it to [Proposed Metrics](../proposed_metrics/README.md) and review it.
- **Approved:** Following a recorded working-group approval decision, move the definition to [Approved Metrics](../approved_metrics/README.md) and update the overview and links.

## Initial review criteria

- A clear purpose, organizational benefit and useful decision or action.
- A calculation reproducible from identified evidence, with external inputs and estimates explicit.
- A defined scope and relevant capability or business context.
- Clear interpretation, limitations, evidence gaps and any applicable thresholds.

Review responsibilities, promotion criteria and the recording of approval decisions remain to be agreed. Publishing these drafts does not approve the workflow or any metric.
